# Convergence backlog

P0 blocks meaningful comparison in the affected setup. P1 marks a major product mismatch, P2 a noticeable mismatch, and P3 polish. S/M/L/XL are relative effort estimates. IDs V01–V10 are the top ten reference-exactness blockers in descending impact, with performance and environment gates below. Dependencies are implementation order constraints.

## Top ten reference-exactness blockers

1. **Hero composition and camera.** The active scene is too front-on and too wide. The heap hero sits near the right edge behind a loud relationship field, while the Stack reads as a separate flat column.
2. **Connector hierarchy and routing.** Bright crossing, bundled, and angular paths capture attention before the causal relation the viewer needs to follow.
3. **Acrylic appearance and edge quality.** Flat saturated plates and jagged outlines dominate. Glass thickness and useful face variation are weak in both quality tiers.
4. **Grounding, floor, shadows and atmosphere.** Hero subjects often appear ungrounded, while array/FAR reflections can mirror whole captions and connector runs. The environment contributes little useful spatial falloff.
5. **Recursion and return composition.** The closest invocations read, but much of the causal chain stacks vertically out of view and the return heading outshouts the recorded value.
6. **Face typography and information hierarchy.** Full signatures, large zone labels, empty plate areas and clipped chip values push important facts down or out.
7. **Focus isolation and Inspector priority.** Focus reaches a legible subject. Unrelated geometry and routes still crowd it, and the Inspector delays exact fields behind chrome and evidence controls.
8. **Semantic zoom and dense composition.** FAR compresses topology into proxies and rails. MEDIUM and CLOSE can expose large unlabeled surfaces and clipped repeated bodies without a clearer task.
9. **Source and timeline treatment.** The controls are useful but sharply bound as IDE chrome, and raw JVM milestone labels overwhelm the small number of events needed to understand the scene.
10. **Motion emphasis.** Many state changes are accurate yet visually similar. A stronger, truthful visual progression should distinguish binding, write, consumption, return, unwind, and completion.
