# Motion audit

**Source:** merged `cd4bd85`. This audit uses actual JVM sessions and the production controls. It reviews chosen events at applied cursors, video of actual replay, and native pointer/keyboard interactions. Capture-ready time includes rendering and camera settle. Video keyframes establish order and visual continuity, with no physical-frame cadence claim.

## Motion settings, zoom and qualification

Source timing defaults include 600 ms Run framing, 900 ms Focus and Home, 300 ms crossfade, 250 ms seek settle, and 250–700 ms semantic steps depending on kind. The live transition layer caps pending steps at 8 and expires stale live motion at 750 ms. These are configured durations, separate from browser wall time and measured command-to-composition latency. A bounded quiet seek and lack of an arrow for unsupported provenance are both intended.

The guided cases and manually driven views kept recorded prefix truth while the camera changed. Eleven equivalent Array Home checkpoints across fresh Runs fixed the same center, distance and cursor. The tested control paths included user interruption, but the samples do not establish all interruption timing or every navigation input combination on other GPUs. The [performance baseline](PERFORMANCE-BASELINE.md) reports a separate whole-render probe and explicit CPU/GPU timing boundaries.

Visual motion acceptance remains open. The current flow can be followed when the scene is sparse and when one knows where to look. The dominant animation often reads as updates to individual panels and wires instead of a continuous causal action. [V02 and V10](BACKLOG.md) rank the visual emphasis and staging work.
